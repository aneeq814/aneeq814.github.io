About me

Designing interfaces, defending ideas

Somewhere between a whiteboard full of wireframes and a terminal full of logs — that is where I like to work.

I'm Muhammad Aneeq Khan, a first-semester student at the National University of Sciences & Technology (NUST). My work sits on two pillars: designing interfaces that feel effortless, and understanding how intelligent systems can be attacked — so that they can be defended.

I'm currently exploring the integration of Artificial Intelligence with the cyber world: how AI can strengthen defence, how attackers could misuse it, and how good design keeps the human in control of both. Design taught me empathy; security taught me scepticism. Together they make me a better problem solver.

I genuinely enjoy helping people — untangling a messy problem, simplifying a confusing flow, or explaining a technical concept in plain language. No drama, no over-engineering: just a clean solution that works and doesn't break anything on the way.
