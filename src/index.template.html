manifest

complexity is not a feature. clean by default. secure by design.

Good interfaces are quiet. When a screen works, nobody notices it — they just get on with what they came to do. That is the standard I design towards: no clutter, no guessing, no friction left behind.

Security is the same craft. The best defence is the one that never has to announce itself. Thinking like an attacker early is far cheaper than cleaning up later, so I build with that mindset from the first sketch.

I am a UI/UX designer and an aspiring cybersecurity enthusiast at NUST, currently exploring the integration of Artificial Intelligence with the cyber world — how intelligence can strengthen defences, and how to keep it from becoming a new attack surface.

Design taught me empathy. Security taught me scepticism. Both keep me honest, and both are just problem solving — which is the part I enjoy most.

just solid work. for interfaces, intelligence and the cyber world.

how i work

Human instinct, machine assistance. I sketch and reason the old way — whiteboard, wireframes, too many sticky notes — and use AI where it genuinely helps: faster research, more variants, cleaner handover.

Every decision gets judged for two things: does it make the interface simpler, and does it leave the system safer than it found it? If a solution fails either test, it goes back on the board.

And I genuinely like helping people — untangling a messy flow, explaining a technical idea in plain language, or quietly fixing the thing that was annoying everyone.

human-centred design. machine-assisted defence.
