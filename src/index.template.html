02 / MANIFEST — OBJECTIVE

Complexity is not a feature.

A working philosophy, extracted from the subject

Operator notesLog 001

Good interfaces are quiet. When a screen works, nobody notices it — they simply get on with what they came for. That is the bar: no clutter, no guessing, no friction left behind.

Security is the same craft. The best defence is the one that never announces itself. Thinking like an attacker early is far cheaper than cleaning up later, so I design with that mindset from the very first sketch.

I'm a UI/UX designer and an aspiring cybersecurity enthusiast at NUST, currently exploring the integration of Artificial Intelligence with the cyber world — building intelligence that strengthens defence, and guarding it so it never becomes a new attack surface.
